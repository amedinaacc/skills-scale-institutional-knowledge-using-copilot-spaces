# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Lead owns the strategic direction of product initiatives, aligns cross-functional stakeholders, and ensures executive visibility on strategic outcomes. They bridge the Product Manager and project sponsorship layers, making critical go/no-go decisions at project gates.

### Responsibilities
- Align stakeholders on product strategy and priorities
- Approve go/no-go decisions at project gates (initiation, planning, release)
- Escalate cross-team dependencies and resource conflicts
- Communicate strategic outcomes to executives and sponsors
- Support Product Managers in defining success metrics and roadmap
- Remove organizational blockers impeding project progress

### Goals
- Deliver strategic initiatives on time and aligned with business objectives
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and executive confidence
- Remove blockers and cross-team dependencies
- Build sustainable delivery practices across the organization

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Product Manager**: Provides strategic guidance on prioritization and ensures alignment with business objectives
- **With Project Manager**: Reviews milestone gates and escalates business-level risks
- **With Developers/Technical Lead**: Clarifies strategic intent and trade-offs to guide technical decisions
- **With Stakeholders & Sponsors**: Acts as primary communicator for strategic progress and decisions

### Typical Communication
- Weekly PM + PdM + PL sync
- Monthly executive steering committee
- Ad-hoc escalation and cross-team coordination
- Milestone gate reviews and decision approvals

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Lead defines quality standards, validates acceptance criteria, and ensures products meet release readiness requirements. They collaborate with developers and product teams to build quality into every delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and acceptance criteria validation approach
- Create and maintain test plans and cases
- Execute manual and automated testing for releases
- Identify and document quality issues with severity and priority
- Participate in sprint planning to estimate testing effort
- Validate Definition of Done before marking items complete
- Establish and monitor quality metrics and trends

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality, reliable software
- Catch issues early to reduce post-release defects
- Maintain clear quality metrics and visibility
- Enable fast, confident releases with minimal risk

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Collaborates on test design and ensures testability of code
- **With Product Manager**: Validates acceptance criteria and confirms feature readiness
- **With Project Manager**: Reports on quality status and release readiness
- **With Product Lead**: Provides quality assurance sign-off for release gates

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- QA review gates before PR merge and release
- Quality metrics and trend reporting
- Design and acceptance criteria reviews

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Lead/Architect provides technical direction, identifies architectural risks and dependencies, and supports technical estimation. They ensure the team's technical decisions align with long-term system goals and organizational standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design principles
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and mitigation strategies
- Support developers in complex technical decisions and design reviews
- Assist in estimating technical complexity and effort
- Ensure alignment with organizational technology standards and best practices
- Collaborate on performance, scalability, and security requirements
- Mentor team members on technical excellence

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and risk in the system
- Enable sustainable development velocity
- Build team technical capability and knowledge

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Provides technical guidance, mentoring, and architecture reviews
- **With Project Manager**: Communicates technical risks and timeline implications
- **With Product Manager**: Advises on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Defines technical requirements for testing and quality assurance

### Typical Communication
- Code reviews and design discussions
- Technical design docs and architecture decisions
- Weekly technical sync meetings
- Sprint planning for estimation support

---

## Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
Engineering Manager manages team capacity, career development, and technical hiring. They ensure sustainable delivery velocity while fostering a high-performing, engaged engineering team.

### Responsibilities
- Manage team capacity planning and workload distribution
- Support career development and growth for team members
- Conduct hiring and onboarding for new engineers
- Address performance and interpersonal issues
- Advocate for team needs and resource requirements
- Foster psychological safety and continuous learning culture
- Ensure team well-being and prevent burnout

### Goals
- Build a high-performing, engaged engineering team
- Maintain sustainable delivery velocity
- Reduce turnover and strengthen team retention
- Support individual career growth and development

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Project Manager**: Communicates team capacity and resource availability
- **With Developers/Technical Lead**: Supports team development and removes blockers
- **With Product Lead**: Advocates for team needs and sustainable pacing
- **With HR/Talent**: Partners on hiring and development initiatives

### Typical Communication
- One-on-one meetings with team members
- Team retrospectives and all-hands meetings
- Resource planning and capacity discussions
- Career development and feedback conversations

---

## Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Stakeholders represent diverse organizational groups (executives, business owners, support teams, sales, customers) with varying communication needs and decision authority. They provide inputs, approvals, and constraints to guide project direction and success.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and requirements
- Approve or gate key project decisions
- Supply customer insights and feedback
- Communicate project value to their departments or customers
- Identify cross-team dependencies and integration needs
- Escalate issues that impact their areas of responsibility

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business and customer value
- Maintain alignment with organizational priorities
- Reduce surprises and enable smooth project delivery
- Support project success through their areas of influence

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Product Lead**: Receives strategic updates and provides executive guidance
- **With Project Manager**: Receives status updates and communicates blockers or new constraints
- **With Product Manager**: Provides requirements and validates solution direction
- **With Developers**: Provides subject matter expertise and use case validation

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or monthly stakeholder status updates
- Project kick-off and gate review meetings
- Ad-hoc escalation and issue resolution
- Shared project dashboards and documentation

---

## Sponsor/Executive Champion

### Role Summary
Sponsor/Executive Champion provides air cover for the project, approves resources, and holds escalation authority for business-critical issues. They ensure executive alignment and remove organizational barriers to project success.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project funding and resource allocation
- Provide executive visibility and air cover for the team
- Escalate business-critical issues to executive leadership
- Ensure alignment with corporate strategy and priorities
- Remove organizational blockers impeding project progress
- Validate business case and success metrics

### Goals
- Ensure strategic initiatives succeed and deliver expected business value
- Remove executive-level blockers and organizational barriers
- Maintain executive alignment and stakeholder confidence
- Support team morale and sustainability

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Product Lead**: Partners on strategic direction and executive communication
- **With Project Manager**: Reviews major escalations and provides authority for decisions
- **With Stakeholders**: Aligns on business priorities and resource trade-offs
- **With Team**: Provides visible support and celebrates successes

### Typical Communication
- Monthly executive steering committee meetings
- Ad-hoc escalation resolution for critical issues
- Quarterly business reviews and strategic planning
- Executive visibility and announcement communications
